Reading

On a small repo the harness context costs more tokens than orientation saves, and wall time lands within variance in most cells. It should pay off where repo-orientation cost dwarfs harness context (large unfamiliar codebases) — that bed is still untested. n=3, free-tier queueing noise visible (362s outlier). Full method, probe log (20 models tried, 2 live), and all 36 raw rows: research/findings/ab-results.md.
