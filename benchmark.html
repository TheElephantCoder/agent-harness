Reading

On a small repo the harness context costs more tokens than orientation saves; on a 200-file bed it wins tokens on build/fix tasks (-13/-14%). Wall time lands within variance in most cells. n=3 throughout, free-tier queueing noise visible. Full method, probe log (30 models tried, 6 live), and all raw rows: docs/benchmarks.md.
